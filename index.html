01About

I'm a biomedical engineer and AI researcher, now a Senior AI Engineer at Quantivly, an AI startup working on radiology operations. Before that, I worked on AI for intravascular ultrasound at Philips Image Guided Therapy Devices, and on quantitative MRI as a postdoctoral fellow at Cleveland Clinic.

My PhD at Case Western Reserve University focused on 3D ultrasound: I built an imaging system for the eye from the hardware up, then wrote the deep learning to segment, enhance and measure what it saw. That project taught me what I still care about most: a model only matters when it fits the workflow of the person who has to trust it.

Today I work across the stack, from data pipelines and model training to LLM-powered tools and production deployment, with a strong bias toward things that are measurable, maintainable and safe.
