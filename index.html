About Me

I am a biomedical engineer and AI researcher, currently a Senior AI Engineer at Quantivly Inc., where I develop AI methods for radiology operations. Previously, I was an AI Engineer and then an AI Scientist at Philips, working on AI-enabled intravascular ultrasound (IVUS), and a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the Lerner Research Institute, Cleveland Clinic, in Dr. Xiaojuan Li and Dr. Kunio Nakamura's lab, where I worked on accelerated quantitative MRI of the knee.

I completed my PhD in Biomedical Engineering at Case Western Reserve University under the supervision of Dr. David L. Wilson. My dissertation, 3D Ultrasound Imaging of the Eye, covered the design of 3D ultrasound imaging systems for the anterior segment and the whole eye, together with deep learning methods for image enhancement, segmentation and volumetric assessment.

Before that, I worked as a computer vision researcher at Semion. I completed my Bachelors and Masters degrees in Electrical and Electronic Engineering at Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology, where I was advised by Dr. Celia Shahnaz.

Projects

All Medical Imaging Speech Processing Biosignal Processing
  • Accelerated quantitative MRI of the knee

    We developed clinically feasible whole-knee T1ρ and T2 mapping in under 3 minutes, combining accelerated imaging with deep learning based automated analysis.

  • 3D ultrasound imaging of the eye

    We developed a 3D ocular ultrasound system (3D-Ultrasound BioMicroscopy or 3D-UBM) capable of imaging ocular structures, applying deep learning segmentation and GAN-based image enhancement, and automating biometric measurements to investigate disease and trauma-related changes and treatment outcomes.

  • Microvascular imaging with non-contrast ultrasound

    We proposed an end-to-end deep learning approach for microvessel imaging using non-contrast ultrasound, which efficiently removes tissue clutter, extracts blood signals, and reconstructs microvessels, demonstrating promising results and potential for adaptation to various scenarios.

  • Speech enhancement using traditional and deep learning approaches

    We developed speech enhancement methods based on wavelet domain features using decision directed approach and generative models.

  • Abnormality Detection and Localization in Chest X-Rays using Deep Convolutional Neural Networks

    We explored the performance of different convolutional neural network architectures in disease classification, organ segmentation and localization in chest x-ray images.

  • Sleep Apnea Detection from EEG Signal Analysis

    We developed a sleep apnea detection approach from spectral features of EEG signals.
