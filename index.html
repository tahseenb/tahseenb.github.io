About

I am a biomedical engineer and AI researcher. I am currently a Senior AI Engineer at Quantivly Inc., where I develop AI methods for radiology operations. Previously, I was an AI Engineer and subsequently an AI Scientist at Philips Image Guided Therapy Devices, where I worked on AI for intravascular ultrasound, and a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at Cleveland Clinic, where I worked on accelerated quantitative MRI of the knee.

I received my PhD in Biomedical Engineering from Case Western Reserve University under the supervision of Prof. David L. Wilson. My dissertation, 3D Ultrasound Imaging of the Eye, encompassed the design of 3D ultrasound imaging systems for the anterior segment and the whole eye, together with deep learning methods for image enhancement, segmentation and volumetric assessment.

Research interests Medical image analysis Image enhancement Segmentation Quantitative MRI 3D ultrasound Generative models Clinical translation of AI
